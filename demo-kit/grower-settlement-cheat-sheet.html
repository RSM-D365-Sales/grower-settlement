Before you walk up

  • Open the Dashboard and press Current period. The week shown should be this week or last. Then pick P40 · Sep 27 – Oct 3 in the period dropdown: Product received should read 286.9K lb from 112 receipts and the weekly settlement run should list 20 settlements, all Posted. If P40 is empty, the site was last deployed before that week; see "If something goes wrong".
  • Leave the dashboard on P40. The app remembers the last period you viewed in this browser, so every walkthrough starts where you left it.
  • Browser at 100% zoom, window at least 1280 px wide. If the sidebar is missing, someone collapsed it: the ☰ button at the top left brings it back.
  • Presenter note: every grower, customer, settlement and figure is Bluestem's demo set. Never describe it as a live tenant or as postings executed in an ERP.
Don't open Traceability or Administration, and don't press Email to grower (demo). The first two are one-line placeholders for later phases. The email button downloads the PDF and shows a toast that says nothing was sent, which stops the story cold. Everything else is safe: nothing in this build writes anywhere. Download PDF saves a real statement file to the laptop, so use it once per conversation, not once per click. Inbound Gate scans live only until you leave the page.

The story in thirty seconds

Bluestem Fresh Produce packs and ships fruit and vegetables from twenty contract growers. Every grower has a flat-rate contract, a price per pound received, and eight of them also have a commission contract, where Bluestem sells the fruit and keeps a percentage. Trucks are read at the gate and weighed, each receipt carries its lot, contract and D365 purchase order, and each week closes on Saturday with one settlement per grower. For pooled crops the growers share the pool's sales, less packaging and repacking, less the advances Bluestem fronted them. The grower gets a statement; D365 gets the vendor invoice and the payment.

Questions you will get

Is this actually talking to D365?

Not in the booth. This build has its demo data baked in and no backend behind it. The product is designed with Dynamics 365 F&SC as the financial system of record: vendors and items are read from it, each receipt creates a D365 purchase order, and an approved settlement becomes a pending vendor invoice and a payment journal. The ST0000xx settlements are converted from a D365 grower-accounting export, which is why their numbers look like D365 numbers.

Why not keep the contracts in D365 as purchase agreements?

Purchase agreements model a price and a quantity commitment. They cannot hold a commission percentage, a pool of growers, or the link from a sale back to the receipt it came from. So the app owns contracts, pools and the settlement calculation, and D365 stays the system of record for vendors, items, purchase orders, invoices and payments.

How is a commission settlement worked out?

From the sales invoice back to the lots that were sold, back to the receipts and the grower's contract line. Net revenue less the company's commission is the grower's. In this build the match is by commodity or item, because the lot-level trace ledger is a later phase; the statement footnote says so.

How does a pool split the money?

Net revenue is added up across every member's lots, then shared by each grower's contribution, by quantity received unless you configure it by value. ST000078 shows the result: three growers, one line each, with their own deductions and advances.

How does the grower actually get paid?

On approval the settlement creates a pending vendor invoice in D365, one per grower, and payment runs through the standard vendor payment journal. Settled lines are flagged so they cannot be settled twice.

Is the scale real?

On this screen the RFID reader and the truck scale are simulated. In production they sit behind a small agent at the receiving station, because a browser cannot talk to a scale directly. The screen and the gross, tare, net capture stay the same.

Who is allowed to see settlements?

Accountant and Admin only. Roles come from Microsoft Entra ID and are checked on the server for every request, so hiding a menu item is never the control. A receiving clerk sees the gate and receipts and nothing about what a grower is paid.

What if a posting to D365 fails?

By design every write to D365 goes through an outbox that records the attempt, the payload and the response, with a key that stops duplicates and a retry screen for admins. A receipt or a settlement is never lost quietly.

If something goes wrong

  • The dashboard shows a week with no activity. Press Current period. If you stepped past the newest data the page says which dates the data covers.
  • P40 is empty, or the newest dates are weeks old. The data is generated when the site is deployed and runs from January 1 to that day. When this sheet was written the hosted site's data ended on September 24. Ask Ryan to redeploy (any push to main, or Retry deployment in Cloudflare Pages); it takes a few minutes and brings the data up to that day. Until then, tell the same story on the newest full week the dashboard offers and read the figures off the screen.
  • The sidebar has gone. Press ☰ at the top left. The setting sticks per browser.
  • You see a sign-in page or "API error". You are on a different build. Use the hosted address at the top of this sheet.
  • Nothing loads at all. Check the booth network, and fall back to the looping video while it reconnects.
